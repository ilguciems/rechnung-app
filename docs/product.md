# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Small business teams (2-10 employees) in Germany who need to generate professional invoices compliant with German tax law. These users work in office environments during business hours, often switching between invoice creation and other business tasks. They need confidence that their invoices are legally correct and their financial data is secure.

## Product Purpose

Rechnung App is a local-first invoice generation tool that creates PDF invoices compliant with German tax regulations (Kleinunternehmerregelung, VAT rates, tax numbers). It exists to give small businesses control over their sensitive financial data while providing a fast, reliable way to bill clients and track payments. Success means users can generate a compliant invoice in under 2 minutes and feel confident their data stays under their control.

## Brand Personality

Professional, precise, trustworthy.

The interface should feel technically competent without being cold. Users should sense that the tool respects their time and their data. The tone is direct and clear, never playful or casual, but also never bureaucratic or intimidating.

## Anti-references

- **Complex ERP systems** (SAP, Oracle): Overloaded interfaces with dozens of tabs, buttons, and nested menus. We are focused, not overwhelming.
- **Legacy accounting software**: Outdated visual design, unstyled tables, gray forms from the 2000s. We are modern and refined.
- **Overly casual/gamified tools**: Bright colors, playful elements, unserious tone. We handle money and legal compliance; the interface must reflect that gravity.
- **Faceless corporate portals**: Cold, generic, soulless enterprise UI with no personality. We are professional but not robotic.

## Design Principles

1. **Practice what you preach**: A tool about precision and compliance must itself be precise and compliant in every detail.
2. **Respect the user's time**: Every interaction should be direct. No unnecessary steps, no decorative friction.
3. **Local-first confidence**: The interface should reinforce that data stays under the user's control. No cloud anxiety, no vendor lock-in fear.
4. **Expert without arrogance**: Assume the user knows their business. Provide power and flexibility without condescension or hand-holding.
5. **German tax law is complex; the UI is not**: Abstract the legal complexity into clear, unambiguous form fields and validation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 Level AA compliance required. This includes:

- Semantic HTML structure
- Sufficient color contrast (4.5:1 for normal text, 3:1 for large text)
- Full keyboard navigation support
- Clear focus indicators
- Screen reader compatibility with proper ARIA labels where needed
- Support for reduced motion preferences
- No reliance on color alone to convey information
